Architecture

What changed?

Both paths retain Clifft's factored state and keep exponential storage at 2^k. The change is how a general Pauli action is lowered and executed.

Legacy Clifft: localize, then execute

Pauli localization → SVM bytecode → localized operations

Pauli localization
The compiler synthesizes virtual Clifford gates that turn a multi-coordinate Pauli, such as X0 Z2 Z5, into +/-X or +/-Z on one pivot coordinate.
SVM bytecode
A flat, optimized stream of fixed 32-byte instructions prepared once for all shots: frame-bit updates, one- or two-coordinate array gates, rotations, and measurements.
Localized operations
Runtime executes the synthesized coordinate changes and then a simple pivot rotation or measurement. The resulting coordinate basis is carried forward.
Symbolic Clifft: plan once, execute direct actions

symbolic frame → shared coordinate plan → direct kernels

Symbolic frame
The planner tracks the stabilizer-coordinate basis while noise, feedback, and measurement effects become XOR expressions over sampled Boolean symbols.
Shared coordinate plan
Active and dormant coordinates, promotions, measurement pivots, and symbol dependencies are selected at compile time and reused by every shot.
Direct operations
The same X0 Z2 Z5 action becomes one Pauli-mask descriptor plus a sign register. A kernel updates the paired amplitudes directly, without a localization sequence.

SymFT lineage

Techniques and what the ablations showed

These findings describe the SymFT/SOFT design study and guided which ideas Clifft adopted first.

Techniques

  • Symbolic signs for noise, feedback, and branches.
  • Adaptive coordinates and direct active-Pauli kernels.
  • Packed shots for Boolean and small-state work.
  • Product components for separable active states.

Ablation findings

  • Removing per-shot Clifford work is the base win.
  • Batching dominates at low k and fades as dense work grows.
  • State layout and SIMD kernels dominate larger active states.
  • Product components are narrow but powerful for weakly coupled high-k states.

Short answers

Architecture FAQs

Will legacy go away?

That is the goal: preserve the Python API and replace its implementation after correctness, portability, compilation, and remaining surfaces are ready.

Why is Pauli localization no longer required?

Direct kernels can act on a general active Pauli without first converting it to one axis: rotations use a direct dense pass, while measurements use direct probability and collapse kernels. Localization was valuable for simpler kernels, but its basis changes can add dense passes. Targeted localization remains possible if profiling finds a cheaper case.

How does symbolic Clifft differ from SymFT?

They share the symbolic-coordinate starting point. Current Clifft executes scalar shots, propagates signs through a sparse incremental register file, and keeps one dense active state while integrating Clifft's sampling and query APIs. SymFT additionally packs shots and can split the state into product components; those remain later options for Clifft.

Why is there no new SVM?

The SVM itself was not a measured performance driver. A compact tape may still become an architectural or inspection boundary later.

What about GPU?

Revisit the design around the symbolic plan, with a GPU-specific lowered program, state layout, and batching strategy.
